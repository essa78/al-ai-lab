About Us

Who We Are

AI Lab is a cutting-edge space where students, faculty, researchers, and industry leaders collaborate to solve real-world challenges and shape the future of technology. The JCU AI Lab empowers students to explore groundbreaking innovations in healthcare, robotics, remote sensing, wide area surveillance, and beyond. Our laboratory supports scientific research, student projects, academic publications, workshops, and cooperation with universities and industry.

Our Mission

To advance artificial intelligence research, student success, and workforce readiness while preparing students to shape the future of intelligent technology.

Projects

Current Projects

Project Research AreaDescriptionStatus
Brain-Controlled RoboticsBrain–Computer Interfaces (BCI), Robotics, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Biomedical Signal ProcessingThis project investigates AI-driven brain–computer interface technologies that enable users to control intelligent robotic systems using non-invasive EEG signals. Research focuses on decoding brain activity for real-time control of drones, mobile robots, robotic manipulators, and other autonomous platforms, advancing human–robot interaction and assistive robotics.Active
Immersive AR TechnologyExtended Reality (AR/VR), Computer Vision, Artificial Intelligence, Human–Computer InteractionThis project develops AI-powered augmented reality technologies that combine computer vision, spatial computing, and real-time interaction to create immersive and intelligent experiences. Research focuses on human pose estimation, mixed reality visualization, and AI-assisted decision support for applications in healthcare, rehabilitation, education, and human-centered computing.Active
AI Agents – Teacher Training SimulatorArtificial Intelligence, Intelligent Agents, Natural Language Processing, Educational Technology, Learning AnalyticsThis project develops an AI Agent-Based Virtual Classroom Simulator that enables undergraduate and graduate education students to interact with realistic virtual K–12 learners exhibiting diverse academic abilities, personalities, learning styles, emotional states, and classroom behaviors. The simulator serves as a virtual teaching laboratory where pre-service and in-service teachers can safely practice instructional strategies, classroom management, questioning techniques, differentiation, and intervention approaches through interactions with multiple AI-powered student agents.Active
Vision Guided Robotics Robotics, Computer Vision, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Autonomous Systems This project develops intelligent robotic systems that integrate computer vision and artificial intelligence to perceive, understand, and interact with their environments. Research focuses on vision-guided navigation, object detection and tracking, autonomous decision-making, and human–robot collaboration for applications in assistive robotics, industrial automation, autonomous drones, and smart robotic platforms.Active
AI-Based Facial Expression Recognition Computer Vision, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Affective ComputingThis project investigates AI-based techniques for recognizing and analyzing human facial expressions. Research focuses on computer vision and deep learning methods for facial expression recognition, emotion-related analysis, and real-time intelligent systems, with applications in human–computer interaction, education, healthcare, and other human-centered computing environments.Active
